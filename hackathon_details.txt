Vinxa Live · you build this
The meetings layer. Your team builds it so every conversation becomes a connected, actionable record.
The product brief
Live
The AI notetaker that never misses a word.
Vinxa Live is the meetings layer of Vinxa. It captures speech, transcribes it as people talk, labels who said what, and turns the conversation into a summary, decisions, and action items that can be searched, shared, and sent on to the rest of the enterprise stack. Every track builds the same product promise on a different surface.
01
Capture
Microphone, system audio, or an on-device mic array. Clear recording indicator at all times.
02
Transcribe live
Streaming speech-to-text with words appearing as people speak, not after the meeting ends.
03
Understand
Speaker labels, summary, key decisions, and action items with owners.
04
Deliver
Searchable notes, export to Markdown or PDF, and a way to delete a recording for good.
Required on every track
✓
Live transcript visible while the conversation is happening
✓
Speaker labels (at least "Speaker 1 / Speaker 2")
✓
Auto-generated summary, decisions, and action items when recording stops
✓
Keyword search across saved notes
✓
Export notes as Markdown or PDF
✓
Visible recording indicator and a consent prompt before capture starts
✓
Delete a recording and its transcript permanently

Web
A browser app that records from the microphone or a shared meeting tab, streams live transcription, and produces shareable notes the moment the call ends. No install.
User story
As a team lead on back-to-back calls, I want to open a link, hit record, and get clean notes I can share before my next meeting starts.
Must ship
Record from microphone and from a shared browser tab
Streaming transcript with under 2 seconds of lag
Speaker labels and timestamps on every line
Summary, decisions, and action items when recording stops
Shareable read-only link to the notes
Works on desktop Chrome and Edge
Stretch goals
Highlight a moment live and jump to it later
Ask questions of past meetings ("what did we decide on pricing?")
Multi-language transcription
Send action items to Azure DevOps or Jira through a webhook
Suggested stack
MediaRecorder / Web Audio API, getDisplayMedia for tab audio
WebSocket streaming to a speech-to-text service or a self-hosted Whisper model
Next.js or any modern web framework
An LLM for summaries and action items
Showcase demo
Run a live two-person conversation on stage. The jury watches words appear in real time, then opens the shared notes link.
Provided on site
Cloud credits for speech-to-text and LLM usage, sample meeting audio, and a hosted demo environment.
Think like a product
From a user to a working product
01

Name one real person and one moment where they need notes. Everything else is scope creep.
Output ·

02
Write the one-page PRD
Problem, user, must-haves, what you are not building, and how you will know it works.
Output ·
One-page PRD reviewed by your mentor
03
Build the thinnest working loop
Capture → transcribe → notes, end to end, ugly is fine. Then make it good.
Output ·
First end-to-end recording
04
Test with real speech
Accents, crosstalk, background noise. Measure lag and mistakes; fix the worst one.
Output ·
Latency and accuracy notes
05
Showcase
Demo it live. The product does the talking; the slides only explain the decisions.
Output ·
Live demo + demo video
The rule
If it does not run live, it does not count.
Slides explain decisions; they do not replace the product. Mentors sign off your one-page PRD before the build sprint, and the jury scores what actually works on demo day.

Questions
Before you register
Do we have to build all four versions?
+
No. Each team picks one track and builds that version of Vinxa Live properly. A great single product beats four half-built ones.
Can we use cloud speech and LLM APIs?
+
Yes. Document what you use in your architecture diagram. Offline or on-device transcription is a stretch goal and earns credit under transcription quality and privacy.
We have no hardware at home. Can we pick the device track?
+
Yes. Device kits are provided on request. Request yours when you register so it is ready before the build sprint starts. At least one teammate should be comfortable with basic electronics.
Can I register alone?
+
Yes. Register as a solo builder and we will match you with a team before the event.
Can we start building before the event?
+
You can research, learn the tools, and sketch ideas. All product code must be written during the build window.
What about recording people without asking?
+
Every Vinxa Live build must show a recording indicator and ask for consent before capturing. It is part of the core requirements and the judging rubric.
How does this fit into Vinxa?
+
Vinxa Live is the meetings layer of Vinxa, EVOCS’s enterprise intelligence platform. Your build is judged on its own, but a clear, documented way to pass notes and action items to other tools earns credit under product thinking.
